Weddings & Catering

The tin at the door, the tray on the table.

If you've ever been handed a small box of mithai at a Bradford wedding, you know the brief. We pack to size, label by guest count, and deliver in time for the welcome. Sweet trays, savoury platters, full mithai tables — built around your day, not a fixed menu.

What we cater

  • Wedding mithai trays & guest gift boxes
  • Mehndi night savoury platters
  • Engagement & reception mithai tables
  • Eid, Diwali & corporate gifting (printed tins)
  • Funeral & memorial trays — same-day where we can

Pop in to the BD7 counter to discuss numbers, timings & collection. We don't quote sight-unseen — every order is built to the day.
